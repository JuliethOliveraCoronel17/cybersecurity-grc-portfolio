# Organizational Context

## 1. Organization Overview

The organization is a small company that provides supplies and services to early childhood care centers.

Its activities include the comprehensive supply of these centers, including:

- Cleaning supplies
- Stationery and paper supplies
- Food and market supplies
- Educational materials
- Support related to early childhood care

The organization also follows the parameters and controls established by the contracting entity for the provision of early childhood services, including requirements related to educational support and nutritional follow-up.

## 2. Main Client

The organization's main client is an **organization responsible for contracting and overseeing early childhood care services**.

The organization currently operates primarily through contracts associated with these services.

For confidentiality purposes, the identity of the contracting entity is not disclosed in this portfolio.

## 3. Contracting Model

Based on the information currently available, the organization has obtained contracts through a combination of:

- Contract renewals
- Participation in contracting opportunities

The organization is interested in expanding its participation in new contracting opportunities in order to reduce its dependence on a single client.

A future objective of this project is to explore whether a more structured approach to organizational documentation, compliance and information management could support its readiness for new contracting opportunities.

## 4. Main Operational Activities Identified

The main activity currently identified is the provision and distribution of supplies required by early childhood care centers.

This includes the supply of:

- Cleaning products
- Stationery and paper
- Food and market products
- Educational materials

The organization also provides or supports activities associated with early childhood care according to the requirements established by the contracting entity.

The complete business process has not yet been mapped and requires further analysis.

## 5. Organizational Structure

Based on the information currently available, the manager is responsible for making the main organizational decisions.

The complete organizational structure, distribution of responsibilities and specific roles have not yet been documented as part of this project.

## 6. Information Identified

The organization is known to handle or have access to different types of information, including:

- Contracts
- Information related to child beneficiaries
- Invoices
- Employee documentation
- Financial information

The exact location, storage method, access permissions, retention periods and backup practices for this information have not yet been fully verified.

## 7. Preliminary Observations

The following observations have been identified during the initial exploration of the organization:

### Confirmed / Reported Information

- Employees work using personal computers.
- The organization handles sensitive business and beneficiary-related information.
- The organization currently relies significantly on contracts associated with its main client.
- The organization is interested in pursuing new contracting opportunities.
- The manager is responsible for the main organizational decisions.

### Information to Verify

The following points require evidence or confirmation before they can be considered findings:

- Whether business and beneficiary information is stored on employees' personal devices.
- Whether formal backup procedures exist.
- Where the organization's information is currently stored.
- Who has access to different categories of information.
- Whether access permissions are reviewed periodically.
- Whether information is protected through formal security controls.
- Whether information is updated and maintained according to defined procedures.
- The current status and completeness of accounting information.
- The complete operational process or core business.
- The organization's current supplier management process.
- The documentation currently available for participation in new contracting opportunities.

## 8. GRC Relevance

The preliminary context indicates that the organization handles information that may have significant confidentiality, integrity and availability requirements.

In particular, information related to child beneficiaries, contracts, employees and finances requires further analysis to determine:

- What information assets exist.
- Where they are stored.
- Who should have access.
- What threats and vulnerabilities may affect them.
- What controls currently exist.
- What requirements may apply.
- What improvements may be appropriate.

At this stage, no security gap is considered confirmed unless it is supported by evidence or verified with the organization.

## 9. Next Step

The next phase of the project will be to identify and classify the organization's main information and business assets.

**Planned sequence:**

**Context → Asset Identification → Risk Assessment → Requirements → Gap Assessment → Controls → Action Plan**
