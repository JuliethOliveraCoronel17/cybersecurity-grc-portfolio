# Asset Inventory – MIPYME

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to identify the preliminary information assets that may be important to the organization's operations, confidentiality, integrity and availability.

This inventory is based on preliminary information collected for a cybersecurity GRC learning project. It will be updated as additional information and evidence become available.

## 2. Preliminary Asset Inventory

| Asset ID | Asset Name | Asset Type | Business Purpose | Verification Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A-001 | Contract information | Business information | Manage contractual obligations and client relationships | Reported; details to verify |
| A-002 | Beneficiary information | Sensitive personal information | Support the delivery and management of early childhood care services | Reported; storage and access to verify |
| A-003 | Financial information and invoices | Financial information | Support financial management, billing and payment processes | Reported; storage and access to verify |
| A-004 | Employee documentation | Personnel information | Support administrative and personnel management activities | Reported; access and storage to verify |
| A-005 | Supplier information | Business information | Support purchasing and supplier management | To verify |
| A-006 | Operational performance indicators | Business information | Support performance monitoring and business decisions | To verify |

## 3. Preliminary Analysis of Sensitive Information

Beneficiary information has been identified as a priority asset for further analysis because unauthorized disclosure could affect the privacy of children and their families.

The following details remain to be verified:

- Where the information is stored.
- Which systems or platforms are used.
- Which roles require access to the information.
- Whether access permissions are defined and reviewed.
- Whether backup and recovery procedures exist.
- Whether information is retained and disposed of appropriately.

No conclusion has been reached regarding the effectiveness of the organization's current security controls.

## 4. Evidence and Verification Method

The inventory distinguishes between reported information and information that still requires verification.

- **Reported:** Information described during the preliminary exploration.
- **To Verify:** Information that requires additional evidence or confirmation.
- **Confirmed:** Information supported by appropriate evidence.

An asset being identified does not automatically mean that a security weakness exists.

## 5. Next Steps

The next activities will be to:

1. Confirm the existence and business purpose of each asset.
2. Identify where each asset is stored or processed.
3. Identify the roles that require access.
4. Determine the confidentiality, integrity and availability requirements.
5. Use the verified information to support the subsequent risk assessment.

## Disclaimer

This document is part of an anonymized cybersecurity GRC learning project. It is not a formal audit or an official assessment of the organization. No real beneficiary information, credentials, contracts or confidential records are included.
