# GRC Diagnostic – MIPYME

## Project Overview

This project is a learning case focused on applying Cybersecurity Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) concepts to a small organization that provides supplies and services to organizations working with children, adolescents and older adults.

The organization provides:

- Food supplies
- Stationery and paper supplies
- Cleaning supplies
- Support and services for children, adolescents and older adults

The purpose of this project is to practice a structured GRC diagnostic based on preliminary information and observations about the organization.

## Project Objective

To perform an exploratory GRC diagnostic to preliminarily identify:

- Relevant business processes
- Information assets
- Potential cybersecurity and operational risks
- Applicable requirements
- Possible gaps
- Opportunities for improvement

The project will also explore whether a stronger organizational structure could support the company's preparation for future contracting and bidding opportunities.

## Scope

The initial analysis will focus on five areas:

1. **Governance**
   - Roles and responsibilities
   - Decision-making
   - Organizational structure
   - Documentation

2. **Risk Management**
   - Assets
   - Threats
   - Vulnerabilities
   - Risks
   - Business impact
   - Existing controls

3. **Information Security**
   - Information handled by the organization
   - Where information is stored
   - Who has access
   - Use of personal devices
   - Information protection practices

4. **Third-Party and Supplier Management**
   - Supplier identification
   - Supplier selection
   - Documentation
   - Contracts
   - Compliance considerations

5. **Contracting and Bidding Readiness**
   - Organizational documentation
   - Requirements for potential contracting opportunities
   - Evidence and documentation that may need to be prepared

## Evidence Approach

This project will distinguish between confirmed information and information that still needs to be verified.

- **Confirmed:** Information supported by available evidence or confirmed by a responsible person.
- **To Verify:** Preliminary observation or assumption that requires additional evidence.
- **Confirmed Gap:** A requirement or control that has been evaluated and found to be absent or ineffective.

This approach is important to avoid presenting assumptions as facts.

## Project Methodology

The project will follow this general sequence:

**Context → Assets → Risks → Requirements → Gaps → Controls → Action Plan**

The analysis will be developed progressively as additional information and evidence become available.

## Important Note

This project is a learning case based on preliminary information.

It does not constitute:

- A formal audit
- A certification assessment
- A legal or regulatory opinion
- An official cybersecurity assessment
- A definitive diagnosis of the organization

Sensitive information such as company names, identification numbers, credentials, personal information, financial information or confidential documentation will not be published in this portfolio.
